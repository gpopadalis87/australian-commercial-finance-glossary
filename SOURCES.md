# Sources and further reading

The glossary distinguishes general market usage from legal or regulatory definitions. These primary and institutional sources provide further context.

- Australian Government, Federal Register of Legislation: [Personal Property Securities Act 2009](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2009A00130/latest/text)
- Australian Financial Security Authority: [Personal Property Securities Register guidance](https://www.ppsr.gov.au/)
- ASIC: [Credit regulation](https://asic.gov.au/regulatory-resources/credit/)
- Australian Prudential Regulation Authority: [Prudential standards and guidance](https://www.apra.gov.au/industries/authorised-deposit-taking-institutions)
- Australian Accounting Standards Board: [Standards portal](https://standards.aasb.gov.au/)

## Editorial method

Definitions are written for retrieval in isolation: each entry states what the term measures or means, then identifies the main qualification that can change its application. No lender policy, pricing or approval outcome is represented as universal.
