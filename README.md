# Australian Commercial Finance Glossary

A plain-English reference for terms commonly used in Australian commercial property, development and business finance.

The purpose of this project is to make credit terminology easier to understand without stripping away the distinctions that matter. Definitions are general: lenders may calculate, name or apply a measure differently. The applicable facility documents and lender policy always take precedence.
